Model scope. Earth’s spin and the SPHEREx orbit are visual illustrations only; the spacecraft orbit is not to scale and does not affect the calculations. The model uses an Earth-centred observer and approximate heliocentric Earth ephemeris on J2000 equatorial axes; offsets are tangent-plane arcseconds relative to the reference date. Synthetic exposure stars, pointing error, brightness and apparent image scale are illustrations, not detector pixels or actual SPHEREx observations. The target is stationary in this model; spacecraft motion, target orbital motion, aberration and light-travel time are omitted.
