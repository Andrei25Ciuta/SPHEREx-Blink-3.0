Earth-centred observer. The model uses an approximate heliocentric Earth ephemeris on J2000 equatorial axes. Offsets are gnomonic tangent-plane arcseconds relative to the reference date. Readouts are rounded for this approximate model, not a predicted source position. Spacecraft motion, target orbital motion, aberration and light travel time are omitted.
