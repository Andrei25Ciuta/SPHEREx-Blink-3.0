| --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace and results navigation | Shared skip links, persistent three-theme control, compact research links, zoom and display controls, observation selector, side-by-side cutouts. | Spectra and detector comparison | Existing spectrum and compare modules with offline tests. | Recheck real-data uncertainty and calibration before changing outputs. |

| Results dashboard | Saved validation charts, importable candidate JSON, searchable explorer, centroid evidence and offline report. A JSON export now preserves fit positions and frame keys.
